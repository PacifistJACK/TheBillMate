Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At The Bill Mate, we prioritize your privacy and data security above all else. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our platform to scan, extract, and manage your financial records.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register via Google or email, we collect basic profile details including your name, email address, and a unique user identifier (UID) provided by Firebase Authentication.
  • Financial Document Data: When you upload or scan a receipt, bill, or invoice, we temporarily process the image to extract structured billing data (e.g., vendor name, items, tax, total).
  • Stored Bill Records: We store the extracted text data (the JSON representation of your bills) in our secure MongoDB database so you can access your billing history at any time.

2. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use your data to provide and improve the core functionality of The Bill Mate:

  • To authenticate your identity and secure your personal dashboard.
  • To process document images using artificial intelligence for the sole purpose of extracting your structured data.
  • To display, organize, and calculate metrics regarding your financial history.

3. Data Processing and AI

The Bill Mate leverages advanced multimodal AI (SambaNova AI) to extract text from your uploaded images. We do not use your personal financial data to train public AI models. Image processing is strictly transactional—once the text is extracted and returned to your dashboard, the raw image is not permanently retained on our servers.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your structured bill data is securely hosted on MongoDB Atlas, adhering to enterprise-grade encryption both in transit and at rest. Your authentication credentials and sessions are securely managed by Google Firebase. Our backend infrastructure enforce strict data isolation—meaning your records are fundamentally inaccessible to any other user.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data. We share data only with essential infrastructure providers required to operate the service (e.g., Google Firebase for authentication, MongoDB for database hosting, and SambaNova for AI inference). These providers are bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You retain absolute ownership of your data. At any time, you may:

  • Access, review, and export the billing data stored in your account.
  • Request the complete deletion of your account and all associated billing history from our servers.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data security, please contact our support team. We are committed to absolute transparency regarding how we handle your finances.
